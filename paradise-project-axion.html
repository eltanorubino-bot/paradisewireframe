[ s.001 · The brief ]

Axion Energy needed more than a launch film. They needed a content engine that could run beside the brand for a full racing season.

The problem. A national fuel brand sponsoring a top-flight motorsport team — but with thirty-two race weekends a year, classic campaign cadences fell apart by week three. Either the brand was loud during the launch and silent the rest of the year, or it diluted the message into wallpaper.

What we made. A hero TVC anchored the season — the cinematic centerpiece. Around it, a continuous content system: weekly race-recap edits, BTS reels, driver-POV vertical content, and adaptive cutdowns for every platform. One shoot, one creative core, twelve months of presence.

The result. +12M organic views across channels, a 41% lift in unaided brand awareness in the target segment, and a campaign that didn't just survive the calendar — it owned it. Repeat engagement: 8.4x industry benchmark.
